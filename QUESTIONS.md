# BrightPath Futures — Questions and Answers

These answers use the teacher guide and charity information pack. Where the client has not decided, the answer is recorded as a project assumption rather than confirmed policy.

## 1. Do users need their own login account page to check their monthly membership, or is a simple “Thank You” message on screen enough?

A login account page is not required for the minimum viable product. Show a clear confirmation screen with the selected plan, a fictional membership reference and next steps. A member dashboard is a stretch goal because the client has not decided whether members will have accounts.

## 2. Should supporters be able to pause or cancel their monthly donations directly on the website, or will charity staff handle that manually?

Monthly membership can be cancelled at any time, but the client has not decided whether recurring sponsorship can be paused. For this prototype, show cancellation information clearly and record recurring-sponsorship pause/cancellation handling as an assumption or future feature. Do not claim that an online self-service process exists unless it is implemented and labelled as a prototype assumption.

## 3. When someone signs up for an event, should the app automatically email them a digital ticket, or is a confirmation message on screen enough?

An automatic digital ticket is not required. The prototype may provide an on-screen confirmation and fictional reference, with clear next steps. It must state that registration is not a confirmed ticket unless the charity approves that process. Email tickets are an unresolved client decision and may be treated as a future feature.

## 4. How many total people are allowed to attend physical events, such as the Northbridge Fun Run?

The client has not supplied capacity figures. Capacity and waiting-list logic are optional features. If included, teams must use clearly labelled assumptions rather than presenting invented figures as confirmed charity policy.

## 5. Who at the charity checks and approves new project updates before they get posted on the main website?

The client has not decided this. Teams should record an approval owner and workflow as an assumption, or leave publication approval outside the prototype scope. The information pack identifies Priya as the charity coordinator for events and supporter records, but it does not explicitly make her the content approver.

## 6. How long may personal details remain in the database before deletion?

The retention period is unresolved. The prototype must collect minimum data, explain its use, provide a correction/contact route and include a documented deletion or retention assumption. Demonstrations must use fake records only; teams should not present an invented retention period as approved charity policy.

## 7. Can we design our own custom logo symbol alongside the required text logo?

Yes. Teams may use the text logo “BrightPath Futures” or create an original simple mark. Any custom symbol must be original and must not copy a real charity’s logo.

## 8. Should we test a declined fake test card, or only successful test payments?

Test both successful and unsuccessful flows. The teacher guide specifically requires testing cancelled checkout, declined test payments, duplicate clicks and returning to the success URL. The prototype should clearly display: “Stripe test mode — no real payment has been taken.”
